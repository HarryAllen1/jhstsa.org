# Early Entry Submission Deadline

Select events have an early entry deadline on **18 March 2027 at 5:00 PM**. These entries are submitted on [JudgePro](https://judgepro.registermychapter.com/org/jpwa-tsastate/conf/jpwa-tsastate/student). Check the Washington TSA event page to confirm the required items.

The 5:00 PM deadline is a hard limit. If you are in the middle of uploading a file when 5:00 PM is reached, your upload may be canceled and you may be disqualified.
