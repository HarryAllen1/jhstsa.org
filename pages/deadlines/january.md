# January Mandatory or Qualifying Event Submission

On **21 January 2027 at 5:00 PM**, events with a January qualifying or progress submission will accept entries. For most events, submit through [JudgePro](https://judgepro.registermychapter.com/org/jpwa-tsastate/conf/jpwa-tsastate/student); some events may use a Google form. Check the official WTSA event page for the exact required items.

The 5:00 PM deadline is a hard limit. If you are in the middle of uploading a file when 5:00 PM is reached, your upload may be canceled and you may be disqualified.

Some submissions may receive feedback; feedback availability varies by event.
