# Cybersecurity

Participants respond to an onsite cybersecurity challenge by identifying breaches through Capture the Flag games. Challenges may include exploit development, digital puzzles, cryptography, reverse engineering, binary analysis, and mobile security.

::: warning
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is permitted for this event. The Resources and AI Reflection Form is not required.
:::

## Format

Teams attend an orientation meeting and then have 48 hours to complete the online challenge. Solutions are scored in real time based on successful completion and the time taken.
