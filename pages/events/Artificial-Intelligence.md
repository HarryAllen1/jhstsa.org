# Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Participants demonstrate their knowledge of artificial intelligence concepts, models, and best practices through a test. Semifinalists use a generative AI tool to solve an onsite problem; completed solutions are objectively measured.

::: warning
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is permitted for this event. Follow the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) guidance in the General Rules and Regulations.
:::

## Format

All participants take a one-hour preliminary test individually. The top twelve individuals advance to the semifinal round, where teams have 75 minutes to plan and develop a solution to an onsite problem using a GenAI tool.
