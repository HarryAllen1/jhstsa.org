# Interior Design

Participants develop color samples and material design boards that communicate a cohesive interior design. Entries include a documentation portfolio and design boards, followed by a semifinal presentation and interview.

::: warning
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is permitted for this event. Follow the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) guidance in the General Rules and Regulations.
:::

## Theme

**Tiny Living, Big Impact**

A nonprofit organization is developing a Tiny Home Community for young adults transitioning into independent living. Design the interior of one model home to maximize comfort, storage, functionality, and affordability within a limited footprint. Include a living area, kitchenette, sleeping area, bathroom, workspace, and storage. Demonstrate multifunctional furniture, universal design, sustainable materials, efficient circulation, creative storage, and residential lighting design. The material budget is $28,000.

## Format

Preliminary judging evaluates the design boards and documentation portfolio. Semifinalists have 20 minutes for setup, presentation, interview, and removal of materials; up to two members may present.
