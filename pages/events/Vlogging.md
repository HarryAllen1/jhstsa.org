# Vlogging

Participants create a connected three-episode vlog series around the theme **The Butterfly Effect**: “Small actions can create extraordinary change.” The series explores how one decision, act of kindness, innovation, challenge, or moment can create a meaningful chain of events and lasting impact through authentic experiences, reflection, videography, editing, and storytelling.

::: warning
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is permitted for this event. Follow the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) guidance in the General Rules and Regulations.
:::

## Format

The three episodes may not exceed six minutes total. The preliminary entry includes the video series and documentation portfolio; semifinalists complete an additional onsite video challenge within 36 hours, followed by an interview.
