# Automated Manufacturing Systems

Teams collaborate to design and prototype a sensor-based mechatronic solution to an onsite manufacturing problem. Teams build and program the system, document its logic and operating instructions in an Engineer’s Guide, and demonstrate the system to judges.

## Format

Teams have 20 minutes to set up, 25 minutes to plan, and two and a half hours to build and program their solution. The system must complete at least two autonomous cycles, followed by a five-minute interview.
