# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are prospective and brand-new Juanita High School TSA members who may not know that TSA events are structured STEM competitions. They arrive looking for a plain-language introduction to the organization, the kinds of opportunities it offers, and the path from joining the chapter to competing at State and Nationals.

## Product Purpose

JHS TSA’s website is a welcoming, practical guide to participation in the Technology Student Association. It should market the chapter while helping students make confident, informed first steps: understand events, find an event that interests them, and know what to expect from local selection through larger conferences.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, informative, and inspiringly motivating. The voice is clear and peer-friendly, never gatekeeping or overly corporate. It makes a competitive program feel approachable while honestly communicating the work and ambition involved.

## Anti-references

Avoid intimidating insider language, sterile institutional brochures, dense walls of rules, and generic startup-marketing treatment. Do not imply that prior TSA experience, personal connections, or natural talent determine who belongs.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the next step before asking students to take it.
2. Make competition feel like an invitation to learn, not a test of belonging.
3. Pair factual guidance with moments of momentum and excitement.
4. Keep the chapter’s local support visible within the national scale of TSA.
5. Use playful, purposeful visual flair that remains readable in the existing documentation site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the current documentation site’s readable, responsive patterns. Use semantic headings and landmarks, sufficient color contrast, and diagrams that communicate in text as well as color. Mobile and desktop are equally supported; no interaction may depend on hover alone.
